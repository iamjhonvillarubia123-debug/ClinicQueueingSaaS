TIME-SLOT MODE BUSINESS RULES
Version 1.0
Date: 2026-09-19
Status: APPROVED STANDALONE BUSINESS-RULE BASELINE — READY FOR EXISTING-SAAS RECONCILIATION
Project: Queuing SaaS Project

======================================================================
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
======================================================================

This document records the approved working business rules for the proposed
Time-Slot Mode.

This Version 1.0 supersedes the working Version 0.2 dated 2026-09-18.
It consolidates the approved standalone Time-Slot Mode business rules after
availability-engine and appointment-lifecycle scenario testing.

This document intentionally defines the Time-Slot Mode as an independent
business system before decisions are made regarding:

- integration with the existing Queue Mode;
- database or persistence design;
- backend/API implementation;
- architecture;
- shared or mode-specific frontend UI;
- migration;
- configuration ownership or placement;
- technical status/enumeration design.

Those matters are outside the scope of this baseline and must not be inferred
from this document.

This document preserves the approved standalone Time-Slot Mode business rules
and is ready to serve as the business-rule basis for reconciliation with the
existing SaaS. It is not, by itself, implementation authorization.

======================================================================
2. CORE BUSINESS CONCEPT
======================================================================

Time-Slot Mode is a reservation-priority queue.

A patient books a scheduled appointment time range. The reservation establishes
the patient's normal scheduled priority.

Reservation Time and live Serving Order are separate concepts:

RESERVATION TIME
- represents the patient's current scheduled reservation;
- establishes normal scheduled priority;
- remains visible even when actual clinic operations deviate from schedule.

SERVING ORDER
- represents the actual live order in which patients are served;
- may be managed by authorized clinic staff;
- may differ from Reservation Time because of delays, walk-ins, late patients,
  missed turns, clinic-assisted insertions, overlapping reservations, or other
  clinic operational decisions.

The SaaS protects reservation rights through visibility, warnings, patient
information, and rescheduling. It does not absolutely force the clinic to
follow the scheduled order. Actual serving decisions remain within authorized
clinic operational discretion.

======================================================================
3. PATIENT BOOKING FLOW
======================================================================

TS-B01 — PUBLIC/QR ENTRY

The patient continues to enter the appointment experience through the applicable
Doctor/clinic QR or public appointment experience.

No decision is made in this document regarding technical routing or UI sharing
with the existing mode.


TS-B02 — CLINIC SELECTION

The patient chooses the applicable clinic/Practice Location and selects
BOOK AN APPOINTMENT.


TS-B03 — ACTIVE DATE SELECTION

The patient is shown active dates available for booking for the selected clinic.

Inactive or unavailable dates cannot be selected through patient self-service.


TS-B04 — TIME SELECTION

After selecting an active date, the patient is shown the available appointment
time ranges for that date.

The patient selects the desired available time.


TS-B05 — SERVICES AND BOOKING QUESTIONS

After selecting the desired appointment time, the patient proceeds through the
applicable Services and Booking Questions.

The approved conceptual flow is:

SELECT CLINIC
→ BOOK AN APPOINTMENT
→ SELECT ACTIVE DATE
→ SELECT AVAILABLE TIME
→ SELECT SERVICES
→ ANSWER BOOKING QUESTIONS
→ BOOK APPOINTMENT
→ OTP
→ FINAL BOOKING RESULT


TS-B06 — OTP

After the patient submits BOOK APPOINTMENT, the applicable OTP verification is
performed.

Successful OTP verification verifies the applicable patient/booking attempt.

OTP verification by itself does not reserve or guarantee the originally
selected appointment time.


======================================================================
4. CONCURRENT BOOKING AND SLOT LOSS
======================================================================

TS-B07 — NO DOUBLE BOOKING THROUGH PATIENT SELF-SERVICE

A patient does not obtain ownership of a selected appointment time merely by
selecting it.

If another patient successfully obtains the selected time before final booking
completion, the later booking must not be silently placed into the now
unavailable time.


TS-B08 — SLOT LOSS AFTER SUCCESSFUL OTP IS NOT OTP FAILURE

If the patient successfully completes OTP but the selected time has become
unavailable, the OTP remains successfully verified.

The failure concerns appointment availability, not verification.


TS-B09 — CHOOSE ANOTHER TIME

When the selected time becomes unavailable during booking, the patient receives
an explanatory prompt and a CHOOSE ANOTHER TIME action.

CHOOSE ANOTHER TIME returns the patient to the active-date selection for the
current clinic.

The patient is not automatically returned only to the previously selected date.


TS-B10 — CHOOSE ANOTHER CLINIC AND OTP REUSE

The conflict experience also provides CHOOSE ANOTHER CLINIC.

The accompanying information explains that the patient may choose another
applicable clinic under the same Doctor.

A successfully completed OTP does not need to be repeated merely because the
patient must choose another time or continue through the applicable
same-Doctor clinic selection during the same valid booking continuation.

The security/expiration duration of that verified continuation is not defined
by this document and remains a later rule/design decision.


======================================================================
5. STANDARDIZED TIME SLOTS, SERVICE DURATION, AND AVAILABILITY
======================================================================

TS-B11 — STANDARDIZED 30-MINUTE RESERVATION SLOTS

Patient-facing Time-Slot Mode availability uses standardized 30-minute
reservation slots, for example:

9:00 AM – 9:30 AM
9:30 AM – 10:00 AM
10:00 AM – 10:30 AM
10:30 AM – 11:00 AM

The system does not create irregular patient-facing reservation ranges merely
from calculated expected completion times.


TS-B12 — AVAILABILITY DEPENDS ON EXPECTED RESERVED WORKLOAD

Availability of later standardized slots depends on the expected workload
created by earlier booked patients.

The system uses expected Service duration to determine which later standardized
reservation slot may be opened.

There is no separate clinic interval/buffer calculation in the approved
Time-Slot availability model. Any Version 0.1 rule requiring such an interval
or dynamically generated irregular start range is superseded.


TS-B13 — MULTIPLE SERVICE DURATIONS ARE ADDITIVE

Each applicable Service has a clinic-configured expected duration.

When a patient selects multiple Services, their configured expected durations
are added to determine the patient's expected service duration.

Example:

Consultation: 30 minutes
ECG:          20 minutes
Procedure:    40 minutes

Calculated expected service duration: 90 minutes.


TS-B13A — OPTIONAL MAXIMUM BOOKABLE SERVICE DURATION PER PATIENT

The clinic may optionally configure a Maximum Bookable Service Duration Per
Patient for patient self-booking.

Its purpose is capacity protection, including preventing one patient from
selecting enough Services to consume an unreasonable portion or all of the
clinic's bookable schedule.

If no maximum is configured, this optional limit does not independently restrict
the additive Service-duration calculation.

TS-B51 — MAXIMUM SELF-BOOKABLE SERVICE DURATION

When the clinic has configured a Maximum Bookable Service Duration Per Patient,
the additive duration of all Services selected by the patient is compared with
that maximum.

If the selected total exceeds the configured maximum:

- patient self-booking for that Service combination is not permitted;
- the system must not encourage the patient to remove Services merely to fit
  within the limit;
- the patient is informed that the selected Services require clinic-assisted
  scheduling;
- the patient is directed to contact/approach the clinic;
- authorized clinic staff may arrange the Appointment using approved clinic
  operational discretion.

If no maximum is configured, this rule imposes no additional duration limit.


TS-B48 — STANDARDIZED SLOT TOLERANCE RULE

Expected completion is mapped to standardized 30-minute reservation slots using
an inclusive 15-minute tolerance.

When calculated expected completion falls within the first 15 minutes after a
standardized slot begins, including exactly the 15-minute point, that same
standardized slot may be opened as the next bookable reservation slot.

When calculated expected completion is later than that inclusive 15-minute
point, availability moves to the next standardized 30-minute slot.

Examples:

Expected completion    Next bookable standardized slot
9:30 AM                9:30 AM – 10:00 AM
9:44 AM                9:30 AM – 10:00 AM
9:45 AM                9:30 AM – 10:00 AM
9:46 AM                10:00 AM – 10:30 AM
10:15 AM               10:00 AM – 10:30 AM
10:45 AM               10:30 AM – 11:00 AM
10:46 AM               11:00 AM – 11:30 AM

This intentionally permits up to 15 minutes of calculated expected overlap so
that a small expected overrun does not consume an entire additional 30-minute
reservation block.


TS-B48A — PROTECTED NON-BOOKABLE PERIODS

The clinic may define periods that are not available for patient booking, such
as a lunch break.

Patient self-booking cannot reserve standardized slots within such a protected
non-bookable period.

A protected/non-bookable period does not by itself prohibit the clinic from
continuing to operate to finish existing patients.

Authorized clinic staff may exercise approved clinic operational discretion
where an applicable staff override is permitted.


TS-B52 — INDEPENDENT RESERVATION WORKLOAD CALCULATION

Each Appointment's expected completion is calculated from that Appointment's
own reserved standardized-slot start time plus its additive selected-Service
duration.

Expected workload is not chained forward from the calculated completion of the
preceding patient.

This preserves standardized reservation times even where the approved
15-minute tolerance permits calculated overlap between consecutive patients.


TS-B53 — AVAILABILITY RECALCULATION AFTER CAPACITY RELEASE

Cancellation or successful rescheduling immediately releases the affected
reservation and causes affected patient-bookable standardized-slot availability
to be recalculated from the reservations that remain.

A cancelled or moved long-duration Appointment may therefore reopen usable
capacity beyond only its displayed 30-minute reservation slot.


TS-B54 — STAFF OVERRIDE OF CALCULATED AVAILABILITY

Authorized clinic staff may deliberately create or assign a reservation in a
standardized slot that patient self-service considers unavailable.

The system must provide an applicable conflict warning.

The authorized operator may explicitly proceed despite the warning.

The system does not automatically move, cancel, or repair existing reservations
because staff deliberately accepted the conflict.


TS-B55 — EQUAL-TIME DUPLICATE RESERVATIONS ARE CALCULATED INDEPENDENTLY

Where authorized staff deliberately assigns two or more patients the same
reservation start time, each Appointment's expected duration is calculated
independently from that same reserved start time.

Example:

Anna: 10:00 AM + 30 minutes = expected completion 10:30 AM
Ben:  10:00 AM + 30 minutes = expected completion 10:30 AM

The system must not reinterpret the duplicate reservations as a sequential
10:00 then 10:30 schedule.

Both remain legitimate 10:00 reservations, and authorized clinic staff
determines their live Serving Order.


TS-B56 — LATEST EXPECTED COMPLETION GOVERNS FUTURE PUBLIC AVAILABILITY FOR
OVERLAPPING RESERVATIONS

Where overlapping or duplicate reservations produce different expected
completion times, future patient self-service availability considers the latest
expected completion produced by the overlapping workload.

Example:

Anna: 10:00 AM + 30 minutes = 10:30 AM
Ben:  10:00 AM + 60 minutes = 11:00 AM

The relevant expected completion for subsequent public availability is
11:00 AM, subject to the standardized-slot rules.

This calculation does not determine the relative Serving Order of the
overlapping patients.


======================================================================
6. RESERVATION RIGHTS AND NORMAL PRIORITY
======================================================================

TS-B14 — SUCCESSFUL BOOKING CREATES A RESERVATION RIGHT

A successfully booked Appointment creates a real reservation right associated
with the patient's booked time range.

The scheduled time is not merely an estimated queue time.

The clinic is expected to respect the reservation, subject to the clinic's
retained operational discretion.


TS-B15 — SERVICE-DATE CONFIRMATION

A patient may confirm the Appointment through the applicable Service Date
confirmation process following the SMS notification.

A confirmed patient is treated as intending to attend and the clinic is expected
to respect the patient's reserved standardized time slot.

Operational enforcement remains subject to clinic discretion.

For an unconfirmed Appointment, clinic treatment may depend on clinic
operations, but actions that may interfere with the still-existing reservation
require the applicable warning prompts.

An unconfirmed Appointment is not automatically cancelled merely because it is
unconfirmed.

Reservation protection applies to the patient's reserved standardized time
slot. Passing that protected time does not itself cancel the Appointment.


TS-B16 — EARLIER RESERVATION HAS NORMAL SCHEDULED PRIORITY

Among patients who are present/waiting and otherwise eligible for service, an
earlier scheduled reservation normally has greater scheduled priority than a
later reservation.

Early arrival by a later-scheduled patient does not automatically give that
patient priority over an earlier valid reservation.


TS-B57 — EARLY ARRIVAL DOES NOT CHANGE RESERVATION TIME

A patient arriving before the reserved standardized slot retains the original
reservation.

Early arrival does not automatically grant earlier priority or rewrite the
reservation.

Authorized clinic staff may choose to serve the patient early under clinic
operational discretion. Actual early service does not alter the recorded
reservation time.


TS-B17 — CLINIC DELAY DOES NOT ERASE LATER RESERVATIONS

When an earlier consultation takes longer than expected, later patients'
reservations remain intact.

Their original reservation times are not rewritten merely because clinic
operations are delayed.


======================================================================
7. CLINIC DISCRETION AND RESERVATION WARNINGS
======================================================================

TS-B18 — WARN, BUT DO NOT ABSOLUTELY ENFORCE SERVING ORDER

The SaaS must warn authorized clinic staff when an intended serving or insertion
decision may interfere with a still-valid scheduled reservation.

The warning does not absolutely prohibit the clinic from proceeding.

Authorized clinic staff retain operational discretion.


TS-B19 — WALK-INS ARE SUPPORTED

Walk-in patients may be accepted and entered by authorized clinic staff.

The clinic decides whether to:

- serve the walk-in;
- make the walk-in wait;
- insert the walk-in at another operational position.

If the intended action may interfere with a still-valid reserved appointment,
the system warns the operator.

The operator may explicitly proceed despite the warning.


======================================================================
8. LIVE QUEUE
======================================================================

TS-B20 — TIME-SLOT MODE HAS A LIVE QUEUE

Time-Slot Mode retains a live operational queue.

The patient experience includes both reservation information and live queue
information.

Conceptually, the patient may be informed of:

- the patient's scheduled appointment time/range;
- the Queue Number currently being served;
- the number of patients ahead.

Reservation information and live queue information are separate.


TS-B21 — STAFF RETAINS LIVE SERVING-ORDER DISCRETION

Authorized clinic staff may manage the live operational order, including
management of:

- walk-ins;
- late patients;
- inserted patients;
- waiting scheduled patients;
- overlapping or duplicate reservations.

Changing live Serving Order does not rewrite the patient's scheduled
reservation.


======================================================================
9. LATE PATIENTS, MISSED TURNS, AND NEXT
======================================================================

TS-B22 — PASSING THE RESERVED TIME DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY CANCEL

A patient whose reserved appointment time has passed is not automatically
cancelled.

The patient is informed that the scheduled appointment time has passed and may
use the applicable recovery options.


TS-B23 — LATE PATIENT MAY STILL APPROACH THE CLINIC

A late patient may approach clinic staff to determine whether the clinic can
still accommodate the patient.

The SaaS does not promise that accommodation will be possible.

That decision remains with the clinic.


TS-B24 — STAFF MAY ACCOMMODATE OR INSERT A LATE PATIENT

Authorized clinic staff may decide whether and where to accommodate or insert a
late patient into live operations.

If the action affects another still-valid reservation, the system warns the
operator.

Staff retains final operational discretion.


TS-B31 — NEXT MEANS MISSED CURRENT TURN

NEXT is an operational queue action.

When a patient is called but is not available to be served, authorized staff
may select NEXT and proceed to another patient.

NEXT means that the patient was not served when the patient's turn was called
and clinic operations continued.

NEXT does NOT:

- cancel the Appointment;
- complete the Appointment;
- terminate the Appointment;
- prohibit later clinic accommodation;
- prohibit eligible self-rescheduling.

The Appointment remains unresolved.


TS-B38 — MISSED-TURN PATIENT DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY RECLAIM PRIORITY

A patient who missed the called turn and later becomes available does not
automatically displace patients whom the clinic has already progressed to serve.

The patient may:

- self-reschedule into another available time; or
- approach clinic staff for clinic-assisted accommodation.

The clinic determines any subsequent operational placement.


======================================================================
10. NO AUTOMATIC CANCELLATION AND END OF CLINIC
======================================================================

TS-B25 — NO AUTOMATIC CANCELLATION FOR LATENESS OR ABSENCE

The system does not automatically cancel an Appointment merely because:

- the scheduled start has passed;
- the scheduled range has ended;
- the patient is late;
- the patient is absent;
- the patient did not appear when expected;
- the patient missed the called turn.

Cancellation requires an explicit authorized action by the patient or clinic
staff.


TS-B26 — UNRESOLVED APPOINTMENT AT END OF CLINIC

An unresolved Appointment expires when the applicable clinic schedule ends.

The final business/status terminology for this outcome, including whether it is
represented or reported as EXPIRED, MISSED, NO_SHOW, or another approved term,
remains unresolved and must be separately decided.


======================================================================
11. CANCELLATION
======================================================================

TS-B27 — CANCELLATION IS TERMINAL FOR THAT APPOINTMENT

The patient and authorized clinic staff may explicitly cancel an Appointment
through their applicable controls.

CANCEL means the Appointment is explicitly terminated.

After cancellation:

- the reservation is released;
- the Appointment cannot use the continuing Reschedule recovery path;
- the patient must use the normal clinic booking process to obtain another
  Appointment.

CANCEL is not RESCHEDULE.

NEXT is not CANCEL.


TS-B60 — NO PATIENT SELF-CANCELLATION AFTER ACTIVE SERVICE BEGINS

Patient self-cancellation is available before active consultation/service
begins.

Once active consultation/service has begun, patient self-cancellation is no
longer available.

Authorized clinic-side correction or handling is reserved for the applicable
existing SaaS rules during reconciliation.


TS-B27A — CANCELLATION RELEASES THE RESERVED STANDARDIZED SLOT

When an Appointment is explicitly cancelled, its reserved standardized time
slot is immediately released from that Appointment and becomes eligible to be
opened again for reservation, subject to the other approved availability rules.


======================================================================
12. PATIENT SELF-RESCHEDULING
======================================================================

TS-B28 — RESCHEDULE REMAINS AVAILABLE WHILE UNRESOLVED

As long as the Appointment/consultation has not been marked COMPLETED or
CANCELLED, the patient has access to RESCHEDULE during the applicable clinic-day
window when rescheduling remains possible.

This includes an Appointment where the patient is:

- waiting;
- late;
- past the reserved time;
- passed through NEXT;
- otherwise unresolved.

COMPLETED and CANCELLED are terminal for patient rescheduling.

Patient self-rescheduling also stops once active consultation/service has
actually begun, as defined by TS-B59.


TS-B29 — SAME-DAY, SAME-CLINIC RESCHEDULING IS THE FIRST RECOVERY PATH

When the patient selects RESCHEDULE, the immediate self-service recovery
experience first presents available appointment times for the same clinic and
same Service Date.

The broader experience may also provide:

CHOOSE ANOTHER DATE

and:

CHOOSE ANOTHER CLINIC

for applicable clinics under the same Doctor.


TS-B34 — SELF-RESCHEDULING USES AVAILABLE SAME-DAY TIMES

The patient may independently choose another currently available time on the
same Service Date.

The newly selected valid time becomes the patient's current reservation.


TS-B58 — SUCCESSFUL RESCHEDULING REMOVES THE PREVIOUS LIVE PLACEMENT

When a waiting, late, missed-turn, or otherwise eligible patient successfully
reschedules, the patient is removed from the previous live Serving Order
position.

The patient cannot simultaneously retain the previous queue position and the
new reservation.


TS-B59 — NO PATIENT SELF-RESCHEDULING AFTER ACTIVE SERVICE BEGINS

Patient self-rescheduling is available while the Appointment remains eligible
and unresolved, but stops once active consultation/service has actually begun.

Authorized clinic-side correction or handling is reserved for the applicable
existing SaaS rules during reconciliation.


TS-B35 — NO SAME-DAY AVAILABILITY DOES NOT CANCEL THE APPOINTMENT

If there is no available same-day time for patient self-rescheduling, the
system does not create or manufacture availability.

The patient is advised that no appointment times are currently available for
self-rescheduling today and that the Appointment has not been cancelled merely
because no slot is available.

The patient is advised to approach the clinic Secretary if the patient still
wishes to be accommodated that day.


TS-B36 — SECRETARY ASSISTANCE REMAINS AN ALTERNATIVE

An unresolved patient does not have to use self-rescheduling.

A patient who is late, waiting, has missed the called turn, or otherwise remains
unserved may approach the clinic Secretary.

Authorized clinic staff may determine whether and how the patient can still be
accommodated.


TS-B41 — PATIENT SELF-RESCHEDULING MUST RESPECT CALCULATED AVAILABILITY

A patient using self-service RESCHEDULE may select only a time that the system
currently identifies as available.

The patient cannot intentionally create a duplicate or overlapping reservation
through self-service.


======================================================================
13. CLINIC-ASSISTED ACCOMMODATION AND RESCHEDULING
======================================================================

TS-B37 — CLINIC ASSISTANCE IS NOT LIMITED BY PUBLIC SELF-SERVICE AVAILABILITY

The absence of a publicly available self-service time does not absolutely
prohibit authorized clinic staff from accommodating the patient.

Clinic staff may determine that accommodation is operationally possible even
when no public/self-service time is available.


TS-B39 — RESCHEDULING REPLACES THE CURRENT RESERVATION

When an unresolved Appointment is rescheduled, whether by the patient or by
authorized clinic staff, the newly selected time becomes the Appointment's
current scheduled reservation.

The patient UI changes to show the new scheduled time.

The previous scheduled time is no longer displayed as the patient's current
reservation.

The system must eventually preserve rescheduling history for audit, operational
history, and reporting, but the technical persistence mechanism is outside this
document.

When rescheduling succeeds, the previous standardized time slot is immediately
released from that Appointment and becomes eligible to be opened again for
reservation, subject to the other approved availability rules.


TS-B42 — CLINIC-ASSISTED RESCHEDULING MAY OVERRIDE AVAILABILITY

Authorized clinic staff may reschedule a patient to a time that:

- overlaps another Appointment;
- duplicates another patient's scheduled time;
- otherwise conflicts with normally calculated appointment availability.

The system must warn the operator before such a conflicting reschedule is
completed.

The operator may explicitly proceed despite the conflict.


TS-B43 — AUTHORIZED OVERRIDE CREATES A VALID RESERVATION

When an authorized operator acknowledges a scheduling-conflict warning and
proceeds, the resulting Appointment reservation is valid.

The system must not subsequently treat one of the deliberately overlapping or
duplicate Appointments as invalid merely because of the conflict.


TS-B44 — PATIENT UI SHOWS THE ACTUAL CURRENT RESERVATION

Following patient or clinic-assisted rescheduling, the patient's UI shows the
actual newly assigned reservation.

The patient does not need to be shown that clinic staff used an internal
availability override merely because the resulting reservation overlaps another
patient.

Live queue information separately communicates actual operational position.


======================================================================
14. DUPLICATE/OVERLAPPING RESERVATIONS AND SERVING ORDER
======================================================================

TS-B45 — SECRETARY DETERMINES RELATIVE SERVING ORDER FOR CONFLICTING RESERVATIONS

When two or more patients have the same or overlapping reserved appointment
time because an authorized clinic operator deliberately allowed the scheduling
conflict, the reservation time alone does not determine which conflicting
patient must be served first.

Authorized clinic staff determines their relative live Serving Order.

Example:

Anna: 10:00 AM – 10:30 AM
Ben:  10:00 AM – 10:30 AM

Both reservations are valid.

The Secretary may operationally place Anna before Ben or Ben before Anna.


TS-B46 — REORDERING DOES NOT CHANGE RESERVATION TIME

Changing the relative Serving Order of duplicate or overlapping reservations
does not change either patient's scheduled reservation.

If Anna and Ben both hold 10:00 AM – 10:30 AM reservations, both continue to
show that reservation even if the Secretary chooses which patient will be
served first.

Their live queue information reflects the actual Serving Order.


TS-B47 — CLINIC SERVING-ORDER DISCRETION APPLIES TO OPERATIONAL EXCEPTIONS

Clinic operational discretion over Serving Order also applies, subject to the
approved warning rules, to circumstances including:

- overlapping reservations;
- duplicate reservations;
- staff-inserted late patients;
- walk-ins;
- clinic-assisted accommodation.

The SaaS maintains the reservation information and provides applicable
warnings, while authorized clinic staff determines the actual operational
serving sequence.


======================================================================
15. CLINIC DELAY AND PATIENT INFORMATION
======================================================================

TS-B30 — DELAYS MAY CHANGE OPERATIONAL EXPECTATION WITHOUT CHANGING RESERVATION

When earlier consultations take longer than expected, the operational outlook
for later patients may move.

Existing reservations remain unchanged.

A patient's UI continues to identify the patient's actual reserved appointment
time and may separately communicate:

- that the clinic is delayed;
- the Queue Number currently being served;
- the number of patients ahead;
- that earlier confirmed/waiting patients remain ahead;
- the availability of rescheduling when applicable.

The system must not silently rewrite the original reservation merely because
clinic operations are behind schedule.


TS-B40 — PATIENT UI DISTINGUISHES RESERVATION FROM LIVE OPERATIONS

While an Appointment remains unresolved, the patient experience must distinguish
the patient's scheduled reservation from actual live clinic progression.

Information requirements include, as applicable:

- current scheduled appointment time/range;
- current Queue Number being served;
- number of patients ahead;
- whether the patient's scheduled turn has passed;
- whether RESCHEDULE is available;
- direction to approach the clinic Secretary when self-service recovery is not
  available.

This section defines required business information, not final UI layout.


======================================================================
15A. SERVICE CHANGES AFTER BOOKING
======================================================================

TS-B49 — PATIENT DOES NOT SELF-CHANGE SERVICES AFTER BOOKING

After a Time-Slot Appointment is successfully booked, the patient does not
self-edit the selected Services.

The patient-facing experience should reassure/direct the patient appropriately
rather than requiring the patient to alter Services to preserve the reservation.


TS-B50 — CLINIC-SIDE SERVICE CHANGES ARE RESERVED FOR EXISTING-SAAS RECONCILIATION

Authorized clinic-side changes to Services after booking are intended to follow
the applicable existing SaaS rules where those rules are determined to be
shared during the later reconciliation phase.

This standalone Time-Slot baseline does not redefine those existing rules.

If a clinic-side Service change increases expected workload, later patients'
existing reservations are not automatically rewritten. Live operational
conditions and patients-ahead information reflect actual clinic progression.


======================================================================
16. GOVERNING BUSINESS PRINCIPLES
======================================================================

The approved rules establish the following governing principles:

PRINCIPLE 1 — RESERVATION VERSUS EXACT SERVICE COMMENCEMENT

Time-Slot Mode reserves scheduled appointment time. Actual consultation may
begin later because of real clinic operations.

A clinic delay does not rewrite the patient's reservation.


PRINCIPLE 2 — RESERVATION TIME VERSUS SERVING ORDER

Reservation Time determines normal scheduled priority.

Serving Order determines actual live progression.

The two must not be treated as the same business concept.


PRINCIPLE 3 — PATIENT SELF-SERVICE RESPECTS AVAILABILITY

Patient booking and patient self-rescheduling may use only system-calculated
available appointment times.

Patients cannot intentionally create duplicate or overlapping reservations
through self-service.


PRINCIPLE 4 — CLINIC STAFF RETAINS OPERATIONAL DISCRETION

Authorized clinic staff may override calculated availability and manage live
Serving Order when clinic operations require it.

Where an action conflicts with an existing reservation, the SaaS must provide
the applicable warning.

An authorized operator may deliberately proceed despite that warning where the
business rules permit.


PRINCIPLE 5 — UNRESOLVED APPOINTMENTS REMAIN RECOVERABLE

Lateness, absence, passing the reserved time, NEXT, or lack of public
self-service availability do not by themselves cancel an Appointment.

While the Appointment remains unresolved and eligible during the applicable
clinic-day window, the patient may use approved recovery paths.

COMPLETED and CANCELLED are terminal for patient self-rescheduling.


PRINCIPLE 6 — NEXT AND CANCEL ARE FUNDAMENTALLY DIFFERENT

NEXT records that clinic operations proceeded because the patient was not
available when called.

CANCEL explicitly terminates the Appointment.

Implementation must never treat NEXT as an implicit cancellation.


PRINCIPLE 7 — STAFF OVERRIDES DO NOT CORRUPT RESERVATIONS

If authorized staff deliberately creates duplicate or overlapping reservations
after acknowledging the required warning, those reservations are legitimate.

The Secretary determines the live serving order among the conflicting patients.

Reordering does not rewrite their scheduled reservation times.

17. APPROVED BUSINESS FLOW SUMMARY
======================================================================

NORMAL BOOKING:

QR/PUBLIC APPOINTMENT ENTRY
→ SELECT CLINIC
→ BOOK AN APPOINTMENT
→ SELECT ACTIVE DATE
→ SELECT AVAILABLE TIME
→ SELECT SERVICES
→ ANSWER BOOKING QUESTIONS
→ BOOK APPOINTMENT
→ OTP
→ FINAL AVAILABILITY VALIDATION
→ SUCCESSFUL BOOKING


CONCURRENT SLOT LOSS:

OTP SUCCESSFUL
→ SELECTED TIME NO LONGER AVAILABLE
→ EXPLANATORY PROMPT
→ CHOOSE ANOTHER TIME
   → ACTIVE DATES FOR CURRENT CLINIC
   → SELECT DATE
   → SELECT AVAILABLE TIME
   → COMPLETE BOOKING WITHOUT REPEATING OTP DURING VALID CONTINUATION

OR

→ CHOOSE ANOTHER CLINIC
   → APPLICABLE CLINICS UNDER SAME DOCTOR
   → CONTINUE APPLICABLE BOOKING SELECTION
   → NO REPEATED OTP MERELY BECAUSE OF THE AVAILABILITY CONFLICT


NORMAL LIVE OPERATION:

BOOKED RESERVATION
→ PATIENT ARRIVES/WAITS
→ LIVE SERVING ORDER
→ PATIENT CALLED

IF SERVED:
→ CONSULTATION
→ COMPLETED

IF NOT AVAILABLE WHEN CALLED:
→ NEXT
→ APPOINTMENT REMAINS UNRESOLVED
→ PATIENT MAY SELF-RESCHEDULE OR APPROACH SECRETARY


SELF-RESCHEDULING:

UNRESOLVED APPOINTMENT
→ RESCHEDULE
→ AVAILABLE SAME-DAY TIMES FOR SAME CLINIC

IF AVAILABLE:
→ SELECT NEW TIME
→ NEW TIME BECOMES CURRENT RESERVATION
→ PATIENT UI SHOWS NEW TIME

IF NONE AVAILABLE:
→ NO AUTOMATIC CANCELLATION
→ ADVISE PATIENT TO APPROACH CLINIC SECRETARY


CLINIC-ASSISTED RESCHEDULING:

UNRESOLVED APPOINTMENT
→ SECRETARY/STAFF ASSISTANCE
→ SELECT/ASSIGN OPERATIONALLY APPROPRIATE TIME

IF NO CONFLICT:
→ NEW TIME BECOMES CURRENT RESERVATION

IF DUPLICATE/OVERLAP:
→ SYSTEM WARNING
→ STAFF MAY RETURN OR EXPLICITLY PROCEED
→ IF PROCEEDED, RESERVATION IS VALID
→ SECRETARY DETERMINES RELATIVE LIVE SERVING ORDER


CANCELLATION:

UNRESOLVED APPOINTMENT
→ EXPLICIT CANCEL
→ APPOINTMENT TERMINATED
→ RESERVATION RELEASED
→ NORMAL BOOKING PROCESS REQUIRED FOR A NEW APPOINTMENT
